# RetailPulse Nigeria — Business Report (2022–2024)

A comprehensive business analysis report covering 3 years of sales and operations data for RetailPulse Nigeria Limited. This report was produced as the written deliverable accompanying the interactive Excel dashboard project, translating analytical findings into boardroom-ready insights and strategic recommendations for business leadership.

---

## About This Report

This report presents the findings from a full end-to-end data analysis of RetailPulse Nigeria's transaction data covering January 2022 to December 2024. It covers 19,702 orders across 8 product categories, 5 regions, 4 sales channels, and 50 sales representatives, answering 15 specific business questions posed by the company's leadership team.

The analysis was conducted using the JTG Six-Phase Data Analysis Process:
1. Understand the Data
2. Clean the Data
3. Structure and Transform
4. Analyse
5. Visualise
6. Review and Publish

## The 15 Business Questions

**Revenue and Profitability**

1. What is the total revenue generated over the 3-year period, and how does it trend year by year?
2. Which product category generates the most revenue? Which is most profitable?
3. Which region contributes the most to total revenue and profit?
4. Which sales channel drives the most revenue? How does Online compare to In-Store?
5. What is the average order value, and how does it vary by segment and channel?
6. What is the effect of discount on profitability? Does higher discount lead to lower margin?
7. Who are the top 5 and bottom 5 sales representatives by revenue and profit?
8. What is the month-on-month revenue trend? Are there clear seasonal patterns?

**Operations and Customer Performance**

9. What percentage of orders are Completed vs Returned vs Cancelled vs Pending?
10. Which product categories have the highest return rates?
11. What is the average delivery time from Order Date to Delivery Date? How does it vary by region and delivery method?
12. How does Customer Rating distribute across regions, channels, and product categories?
13. Which payment method is most common? Is there a relationship between payment method and order value?
14. How does customer segment distribution change over time? Is the VIP segment growing?
15. Which delivery method has the highest on-time delivery rate? Which has the most late deliveries?

## Key Findings

- RetailPulse Nigeria generated N555M in net revenue across 2022 to 2024 at a consistent 42.67% profit margin, but revenue growth was effectively flat at 0.29% over the full 3-year period
- Electronics drives 58.39% of total revenue, a dangerous category concentration with no secondary category capable of absorbing a downturn
- 1 in 4 orders never completes, representing N99M lost to returns and cancellations across the review period
- WhatsApp Commerce is the only channel growing in both transaction volume and average order value every single year, yet receives the least strategic investment
- The VIP loyalty programme is not working. VIP customers spend virtually the same as New customers despite receiving the highest average discounts
- Standard and Express delivery are both chronically overrunning their service level promises, driving cancellations and return rates
- January is the highest revenue month and Q3 (July to September) is the strongest quarter. December produces no festive season spike due to the Electronics-heavy product mix

---

## Strategic Recommendations

1. **Diversify revenue beyond Electronics** to develop Home and Living and Clothing and Fashion to 15 to 20% revenue share each
2. **Restructure the VIP loyalty programme** by replacing blanket discounts with value-added benefits tied to annual spend thresholds
3. **Fix logistics partner performance** by implementing SLAs and courier scorecards for Standard and Express delivery methods
4. **Accelerate WhatsApp Commerce** by assigning dedicated management, building a WhatsApp-specific catalogue, and targeting 20% channel revenue share by 2026
